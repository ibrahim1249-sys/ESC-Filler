    Why slow, and why deep

    The 3-second lowerThe lowering part (orange on the bar) is where muscles do the most work. Taking 3 seconds keeps them working longer every rep: that's time under tension.
    Full range, every repAll the way down to a stretch, all the way up to a squeeze. A full rep with a light weight builds more than a half rep with a heavy one.
    BreatheBreathe in on the way down, out on the effort. Never hold your breath.
    When to go heavierWhen 15 slow, full-depth reps feel doable on both sets, go up one step next time. Shortening the range to finish reps means the weight is too heavy.
    Rest60–90 seconds between sets. Start with 5 minutes of easy cardio to warm up. Stop if anything hurts sharply.
